## Style-Specific Decorations

### Cute Style
- Hearts, stars, sparkles dominant
- Soft rounded shapes
- Sticker-style elements
- Pastel color palette

### Notion Style
- Simple line doodles only
- Geometric shapes (circles, squares)
- No colorful stickers
- Wobble hand-drawn effect

### Warm Style
- Sun rays, coffee cups
- Soft glowing elements
- Warm-toned decorations
- Cozy, inviting shapes

### Fresh Style
- Plant leaves, water drops
- Clean geometric shapes
- Minimal decorations
- Natural organic feel

### Bold Style
- Exclamation marks, arrows
- Strong geometric shapes
- High contrast elements
- No soft or delicate items

### Pop Style
- Starburst, speech bubbles
- Comic-style effects
- Dynamic shapes
- High energy elements

### Retro Style
- Halftone dots, vintage badges
- Film strip frames
- Aged textures
- Nostalgic elements

### Chalkboard Style
- Chalk-drawn lines and shapes
- Eraser smudge effects
- Mathematical symbols
- Imperfect hand-drawn quality

### Screen-Print Style
- No decorative doodles
- Geometric framing only
- Silhouette shapes
- Typography as decoration
